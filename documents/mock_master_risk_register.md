## Purpose

This Master Risk Register serves as Novara Therapeutics' institutional risk library, capturing risks identified, assessed, and mitigated across all completed and active clinical studies. It is intended as a mandatory reference document for risk assessment generation on all new protocols to ensure consistency, leverage historical learning, and avoid repeat risk failures.

Risk entries are drawn from post-study risk reviews, DSMB recommendations, regulatory feedback, and site performance data across Novara's Phase 1–3 portfolio.

---

## How to Use This Document

When generating a risk assessment for a new study:
1. Cross-reference all identified risks against this register
2. Flag any risk that has **occurred previously** (Occurrence Count > 0) as elevated priority
3. Flag any risk category not present in this register as **novel** — requiring additional scrutiny
4. Apply historical mitigation strategies where applicable
5. Note where historical mitigations **failed** and escalate accordingly

### DOMAIN: PATIENT SAFETY

---

**Risk ID:** MRR-PS-001
**Risk Title:** Drug-Induced Liver Injury (DILI)
**Description:** Elevation of liver enzymes (ALT/AST) beyond protocol thresholds leading to participant discontinuation or serious hepatic injury.
**Historical Occurrence Count:** 4 studies
**Most Recent Study:** NVR-2022-HEP-01 (Phase 1b, hepatology indication)
**Severity When Occurred:** High — 2 discontinuations; 1 SAE (non-fatal); no Hy's Law cases
**Root Cause (Historical):** Insufficient LFT monitoring frequency in early treatment weeks; sites not trained on asymptomatic ALT recognition
**Historical Mitigation Applied:** Weekly LFT monitoring Weeks 1–4; mandatory hepatology consult for ALT >3x ULN; enhanced site training module
**Mitigation Effectiveness:** Effective — no DILI progression to serious injury after protocol amendment
**Regulatory Flag:** FDA issued a Special Protocol Assessment comment on NVR-2022-HEP-01 requesting enhanced hepatic monitoring. Incorporated into amended protocol.
**Applicability Note:** Any study involving hepatic metabolism, CYP3A4-dependent compounds, or GLP-1/GIP receptor agonists should treat this as a HIGH PRIORITY risk given historical precedent.

---

**Risk ID:** MRR-PS-002
**Risk Title:** Hypoglycemia in Non-Diabetic Populations
**Description:** Unexpected blood glucose reductions in non-diabetic participants receiving metabolically active compounds.
**Historical Occurrence Count:** 2 studies
**Most Recent Study:** NVR-2021-MET-03 (Phase 2, metabolic syndrome)
**Severity When Occurred:** Low — mild, self-resolving; no hospitalizations
**Root Cause (Historical):** Concomitant use of sulfonylureas not captured at screening; dietary changes post-enrollment
**Historical Mitigation Applied:** Expanded concomitant medication exclusion criteria; dietary diary for first 4 weeks
**Mitigation Effectiveness:** Effective
**Applicability Note:** Relevant for any metabolic or obesity indication study.

---

**Risk ID:** MRR-PS-003
**Risk Title:** Pancreatitis — GLP-1 Class Effect
**Description:** Acute pancreatitis associated with GLP-1 receptor agonist mechanism; manifests as severe abdominal pain, elevated amylase/lipase.
**Historical Occurrence Count:** 1 study (industry reference; not Novara internal)
**Most Recent Study:** Industry reference — semaglutide Phase 3 SUSTAIN program
**Severity When Occurred:** High — hospitalization required; study drug discontinuation
**Root Cause (Historical):** Class pharmacology; pre-existing subclinical gallbladder disease not captured at screening
**Historical Mitigation Applied:** Gallbladder imaging at baseline; amylase/lipase monitoring; enhanced participant symptom education
**Mitigation Effectiveness:** Partially effective — baseline screening improved detection of at-risk participants
**Applicability Note:** MANDATORY risk inclusion for any GLP-1 or GIP agonist program. Gallbladder disease exclusion criteria must be explicitly stated.

---

**Risk ID:** MRR-PS-004
**Risk Title:** Cardiovascular Events in High-Risk Metabolic Populations
**Description:** MACE events (MI, stroke, CV death) in obese or diabetic participants with pre-existing cardiovascular risk factors.
**Historical Occurrence Count:** 2 studies
**Most Recent Study:** NVR-2020-OB-01 (Phase 2, obesity)
**Severity When Occurred:** High — 1 non-fatal MI (assessed unrelated to study drug); 1 stroke (assessed possibly related)
**Root Cause (Historical):** Inadequate baseline CV risk stratification; participants with undiagnosed hypertension enrolled
**Historical Mitigation Applied:** Mandatory ECG and cardiologist sign-off for participants with BMI >40 or existing hypertension
**Mitigation Effectiveness:** Effective after amendment; no further CV events
**Regulatory Flag:** FDA requested CV risk stratification data in Type B meeting for NVR-2020-OB-01.
**Applicability Note:** Any obesity or metabolic study must include explicit CV risk stratification at screening.

---

**Risk ID:** MRR-PS-005
**Risk Title:** Pregnancy Exposure — Teratogenicity Unknown
**Description:** Inadvertent pregnancy exposure to investigational compound with unknown or incomplete reproductive toxicology data.
**Historical Occurrence Count:** 3 studies
**Most Recent Study:** NVR-2023-MET-05 (Phase 2)
**Severity When Occurred:** Moderate — 3 pregnancies reported; all elected termination; no fetal outcomes assessed
**Root Cause (Historical):** Inadequate contraception counseling at screening; monthly pregnancy tests not performed per protocol
**Historical Mitigation Applied:** Contraception attestation form; monthly urine hCG; site retraining on protocol requirements
**Mitigation Effectiveness:** Effective after retraining
**Applicability Note:** Standard inclusion for all studies where reproductive toxicology is incomplete.

### DOMAIN: DATA QUALITY

---

**Risk ID:** MRR-DQ-001
**Risk Title:** Protocol Deviation — Missed Laboratory Assessments
**Description:** Sites failing to collect scheduled laboratory samples at protocol-specified timepoints, resulting in missing primary or safety data.
**Historical Occurrence Count:** 6 studies
**Most Recent Study:** NVR-2024-MET-07 (Phase 2, ongoing)
**Severity When Occurred:** Medium — affected 12–18% of visits at low-performing sites; required imputation in statistical analysis
**Root Cause (Historical):** Complex laboratory schedules not adequately communicated to site coordinators; EDC visit windows misconfigured
**Historical Mitigation Applied:** Simplified laboratory collection cards; EDC alert system for upcoming lab visits; targeted site retraining
**Mitigation Effectiveness:** Partially effective — reduced but not eliminated
**Applicability Note:** HIGH PRIORITY for any study with a complex laboratory schedule or multi-timepoint safety monitoring. When Protocol and DSMP laboratory schedules are misaligned, deviation rate increases significantly.

---

**Risk ID:** MRR-DQ-002
**Risk Title:** Adverse Event Underreporting
**Description:** Sites failing to capture and report adverse events, particularly asymptomatic laboratory findings not considered clinically significant by investigators.
**Historical Occurrence Count:** 5 studies
**Most Recent Study:** NVR-2023-HEP-02 (Phase 1b)
**Severity When Occurred:** High — FDA Form 483 observation in NVR-2023-HEP-02 inspection; required retrospective AE reconciliation
**Root Cause (Historical):** Investigators did not consider asymptomatic ALT elevations as AEs requiring reporting; site SOPs inadequate
**Historical Mitigation Applied:** Enhanced investigator training; protocol-specific AE reporting guidance document; real-time central lab alert triggers
**Mitigation Effectiveness:** Effective after remediation
**Regulatory Flag:** FDA 483 observation. Incorporated into Novara standard site training package as mandatory module.
**Applicability Note:** CRITICAL for any hepatic compound. Asymptomatic laboratory findings must be explicitly defined as reportable AEs in the protocol and DSMP.

---

**Risk ID:** MRR-DQ-003
**Risk Title:** Primary Endpoint Data Integrity — Weight Measurement Variability
**Description:** Inconsistent body weight measurement procedures across sites (different scales, timing, clothing) introducing bias in primary endpoint data.
**Historical Occurrence Count:** 3 studies
**Most Recent Study:** NVR-2020-OB-01 (Phase 2, obesity)
**Severity When Occurred:** Medium — coefficient of variation >3% across sites; required post-hoc sensitivity analysis
**Root Cause (Historical):** Site-specific weighing procedures not standardized in protocol; calibration logs not maintained
**Historical Mitigation Applied:** Standardized weighing procedure manual; mandatory scale calibration certification; central review of baseline weights
**Mitigation Effectiveness:** Effective
**Applicability Note:** Mandatory risk for any obesity or weight management study with body weight as primary endpoint.

---

**Risk ID:** MRR-DQ-004
**Risk Title:** Drug-Drug Interaction Data Gaps — Concomitant Medication Capture
**Description:** Incomplete capture of concomitant medications at screening and during study, particularly OTC supplements and herbal products metabolized by CYP enzymes.
**Historical Occurrence Count:** 4 studies
**Most Recent Study:** NVR-2022-MET-04 (Phase 2)
**Severity When Occurred:** Medium — post-hoc analysis identified potential CYP3A4 interactions in 8% of participants; not captured prospectively
**Root Cause (Historical):** Concomitant medication eCRF did not prompt for OTC or herbal products; site coordinators not trained to ask
**Historical Mitigation Applied:** Expanded concomitant medication eCRF with specific OTC/herbal prompts; pharmacist review at baseline
**Mitigation Effectiveness:** Effective
**Applicability Note:** HIGH PRIORITY for any CYP3A4-metabolized compound. Historical failure rate is significant.

### DOMAIN: OPERATIONAL

---

**Risk ID:** MRR-OP-001
**Risk Title:** Site Enrollment Delays — Overly Restrictive Eligibility Criteria
**Description:** Enrollment falling below projections due to eligibility criteria that are too narrow for the target population in practice.
**Historical Occurrence Count:** 5 studies
**Most Recent Study:** NVR-2023-MET-05 (Phase 2)
**Severity When Occurred:** High — 3 studies required protocol amendment to relax criteria; 2 required site additions
**Root Cause (Historical):** Eligibility criteria designed from preclinical assumptions without real-world feasibility assessment; no pre-study patient chart review
**Historical Mitigation Applied:** Pre-study patient chart review at all sites; eligibility criteria feasibility assessment before finalization
**Mitigation Effectiveness:** Effective when applied prospectively
**Applicability Note:** Standard operational risk for Phase 2 studies. Particularly relevant when hepatic exclusion criteria may over-exclude the target population.

---

**Risk ID:** MRR-OP-002
**Risk Title:** DSMB Charter Gaps — Missing Safety Review Criteria
**Description:** DSMB charter or DSMP not specifying safety review triggers for known compound-specific risks, resulting in delayed safety response.
**Historical Occurrence Count:** 3 studies
**Most Recent Study:** NVR-2022-HEP-01 (Phase 1b)
**Severity When Occurred:** High — DSMB convened 6 weeks late for hepatic safety review due to undefined trigger criteria; FDA inquiry followed
**Root Cause (Historical):** DSMP prepared before IB finalized; hepatic signal identified in Phase 1 not incorporated into DSMP monitoring triggers
**Historical Mitigation Applied:** Mandatory DSMP-IB cross-reference checklist before IRB submission; DSMB charter now requires signal-specific review criteria
**Mitigation Effectiveness:** Effective
**Regulatory Flag:** FDA raised DSMB charter adequacy in NVR-2022-HEP-01 review. Now a Novara SOP requirement.
**Applicability Note:** CRITICAL. When a new safety signal is identified in Phase 1, it MUST be explicitly addressed in Phase 2 DSMP with defined monitoring triggers before study initiation.

---

**Risk ID:** MRR-OP-003
**Risk Title:** Multi-Site Laboratory Variability
**Description:** Differences in laboratory assay methods, reference ranges, and equipment across sites introducing inconsistency in safety laboratory data.
**Historical Occurrence Count:** 4 studies
**Most Recent Study:** NVR-2021-MET-03 (Phase 2)
**Severity When Occurred:** Medium — 15% of ALT values required normalization; complicated safety data interpretation
**Root Cause (Historical):** No central laboratory specified; local laboratory reference ranges varied by up to 40%
**Historical Mitigation Applied:** Mandatory central laboratory for all safety parameters; local labs permitted for non-critical assessments only
**Mitigation Effectiveness:** Effective
**Applicability Note:** HIGH PRIORITY for any study where hepatic safety monitoring is critical. Local laboratory variability in LFT reference ranges is a documented historical problem.

---

**Risk ID:** MRR-OP-004
**Risk Title:** Site Training Gaps — Protocol Complexity
**Description:** Site coordinators and investigators not adequately trained on complex protocol procedures, particularly multi-step safety response algorithms.
**Historical Occurrence Count:** 7 studies (most common operational risk in Novara portfolio)
**Most Recent Study:** NVR-2024-MET-07 (Phase 2, ongoing)
**Severity When Occurred:** Medium — protocol deviations in 20–35% of sites in first 3 months; corrected after retraining
**Root Cause (Historical):** Investigator meetings focused on efficacy endpoints; safety procedures undertrained; complex stopping rules not simplified
**Historical Mitigation Applied:** Dedicated safety training module; quick reference cards for stopping rules; quarterly retraining calls
**Mitigation Effectiveness:** Partially effective — requires ongoing reinforcement
**Applicability Note:** Standard risk for all Phase 2+ studies. Particularly important when stopping rules involve multi-step decision algorithms (hold/recheck/resume logic).

### DOMAIN: REGULATORY

---

**Risk ID:** MRR-REG-001
**Risk Title:** IND Safety Reporting Non-Compliance
**Description:** Failure to submit IND safety reports within regulatory timelines for serious unexpected adverse reactions.
**Historical Occurrence Count:** 2 studies
**Most Recent Study:** NVR-2021-MET-03 (Phase 2)
**Severity When Occurred:** High — FDA issued a clinical hold warning on NVR-2021-MET-03 for late SUSAR submission
**Root Cause (Historical):** Site-to-sponsor communication delays; sponsor safety team not notified within 24 hours per SOP
**Historical Mitigation Applied:** Automated SAE notification system; 24-hour sponsor safety hotline; mandatory sponsor acknowledgment within 4 hours
**Mitigation Effectiveness:** Effective after system implementation
**Regulatory Flag:** Clinical hold warning received. Novara safety reporting SOP revised and resubmitted to FDA.
**Applicability Note:** Standard regulatory risk. Escalated priority when compound has known safety signals requiring expedited reporting pathways.

---

**Risk ID:** MRR-REG-002
**Risk Title:** Informed Consent Inadequacy — Known Risk Disclosure
**Description:** Informed consent form not adequately disclosing known risks identified from prior studies or Phase 1 findings.
**Historical Occurrence Count:** 3 studies
**Most Recent Study:** NVR-2022-HEP-01 (Phase 1b)
**Severity When Occurred:** High — IRB required emergency consent amendment after Phase 1 hepatic signal identified post-enrollment initiation
**Root Cause (Historical):** ICF finalized before Phase 1 safety data available; no process to update ICF when new signals emerge
**Historical Mitigation Applied:** ICF update SOP triggered by any new safety signal; participant re-consent required within 30 days
**Mitigation Effectiveness:** Effective
**Applicability Note:** CRITICAL for NVR programs where Phase 1 signals emerge during Phase 2 enrollment. ICF must explicitly disclose the 12.5% ALT elevation signal observed at therapeutic dose.

---

**Risk ID:** MRR-REG-003
**Risk Title:** 21 CFR Part 11 Non-Compliance — Electronic Records
**Description:** Electronic data capture systems not validated for 21 CFR Part 11 compliance, risking data integrity findings at FDA inspection.
**Historical Occurrence Count:** 1 study
**Most Recent Study:** NVR-2019-ONC-01 (Phase 1)
**Severity When Occurred:** High — FDA inspection finding; required retrospective validation documentation
**Root Cause (Historical):** EDC vendor changed system version mid-study without revalidation; audit trail gaps identified
**Historical Mitigation Applied:** Mandatory EDC validation package review before study start; change control SOP for mid-study system updates
**Mitigation Effectiveness:** Effective
**Applicability Note:** Standard regulatory risk. Particularly relevant for electronic safety alert and laboratory notification systems.
