## Approved Risk Register

---

### RSK-001 | Patient Safety | HIGH
**Risk Title:** Drug-Induced Liver Injury (DILI) — Transient ALT Elevation
**Description:** Known Phase 1 signal of 12.5% ALT elevation at 15mg dose. Risk of DILI progression if monitoring is insufficient or sites miss asymptomatic elevations.
**Likelihood:** Medium
**Impact:** High
**Overall Rating:** HIGH
**Current Status:** ACTIVE — MONITORING
**Trigger Events to Date:** 4 participants with ALT 1.5–2x ULN at Week 4; all resolved by Week 8 without dose modification. No ALT >3x ULN observed.
**Mitigation in Place:** Enhanced LFT monitoring at Weeks 2, 4, 8, 12; site hepatic safety training completed at all 18 active sites
**Owner:** Medical Monitor / DSMB
**Last Updated:** February 15, 2025
**Next Review Trigger:** Any ALT >3x ULN OR at scheduled May review

---

### RSK-002 | Data Quality | HIGH
**Risk Title:** Cross-Document Laboratory Schedule Mismatch
**Description:** Protocol and DSMP had conflicting LFT schedules at study start. Sites were following the DSMP schedule, missing Week 4 LFT timepoint.
**Likelihood:** High
**Impact:** Medium
**Overall Rating:** HIGH
**Current Status:** MITIGATED — MONITORING
**Trigger Events to Date:** 11 participants missed Week 4 LFT due to DSMP/Protocol discrepancy before amendment
**Mitigation in Place:** Protocol Amendment 1 issued January 2025; DSMP updated; all sites retrained; EDC updated with correct visit windows
**Owner:** Clinical Operations / Quality Assurance
**Last Updated:** February 15, 2025
**Next Review Trigger:** SDV findings at next monitoring visit

---

### RSK-003 | Patient Safety | HIGH
**Risk Title:** Pancreatitis — GLP-1 Class Effect
**Description:** Low but known class risk for GLP-1 receptor agonists. Manifests as severe abdominal pain with amylase/lipase elevation.
**Likelihood:** Low
**Impact:** High
**Overall Rating:** HIGH
**Current Status:** ACTIVE — MONITORING
**Trigger Events to Date:** None. Zero pancreatitis cases to date.
**Mitigation in Place:** Amylase/lipase at baseline and Weeks 12, 24, 36; participant wallet card with symptom guidance; site training completed
**Owner:** Medical Monitor
**Last Updated:** February 15, 2025
**Next Review Trigger:** Any pancreatitis case OR amylase/lipase >3x ULN

---
