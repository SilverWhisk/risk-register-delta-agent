## DSMB April 2025 Review — Key Outcomes

The DSMB convened April 22, 2025 for the second interim safety review.

**Findings:**
- DSMB expressed concern regarding the **increasing rate of ALT elevations** since the February review
- Recommended enhanced hepatic monitoring — unscheduled LFT at Week 20 added for all active participants
- Recommended medical monitor review of all ALT >2x ULN cases within 48 hours going forward
- No recommendation to stop or pause the study
- Next DSMB review scheduled July 15, 2025

## Safety Events — Review Period

### Hepatic Events (ALT/AST Elevations)
- **Total participants with ALT elevation since study start:** 18 (12.6% of enrolled — consistent with Phase 1 signal)
- **New ALT elevations since February review:** 11 additional participants
- **ALT 1.5–3x ULN (asymptomatic):** 14 participants — all managed per protocol; 9 resolved, 5 ongoing monitoring
- **ALT 3–5x ULN:** 3 participants
  - Participant 047 (Site 07): ALT 4.2x ULN at Week 20 — dose held; resolved to 1.8x ULN at Week 22 recheck; dose resumed
  - Participant 089 (Site 12): ALT 3.6x ULN at Week 16 — dose held; fully resolved at Week 18; dose resumed
  - Participant 112 (Site 19): ALT 3.1x ULN at Week 24 — dose held; pending recheck at Week 26
- **ALT >5x ULN:** 1 participant
  - **Participant 134 (Site 03): ALT 6.8x ULN at Week 22** — SERIOUS ADVERSE EVENT reported. Study drug permanently discontinued. Participant referred to hepatology. Follow-up ongoing. No symptoms of hepatitis or jaundice. Sponsor medical monitor assessment: possibly related to study drug. FDA 15-day safety report submitted May 2, 2025.
- **Hy's Law criteria met:** 0 participants

### Pancreatitis
- **1 confirmed case of acute pancreatitis**
  - Participant 078 (Site 11): Hospitalized Week 24 with severe abdominal pain; amylase 4.8x ULN, lipase 5.2x ULN; imaging confirmed acute pancreatitis
  - Study drug permanently discontinued
  - SAE reported; assessed as possibly related to study drug
  - Patient recovered fully; discharged after 4 days

### Cardiovascular Events
- 1 non-fatal myocardial infarction (Participant 056, Site 08, Week 26)
- Assessed as unlikely related to study drug (participant had pre-existing CAD not captured at screening)
- SAE reported; DSMB notified

### Pregnancies
- **1 pregnancy reported**
  - Participant 101 (Site 15): Positive urine hCG at Week 20 visit
  - Study drug discontinued immediately
  - Participant counseled; pregnancy outcome follow-up ongoing
  - SAE reported per protocol

## Laboratory & Data Quality

### Central Laboratory Compliance
- Central laboratory now in use at all 23 active sites for safety parameters
- 3 sites with previously non-standard ALT reference ranges now fully migrated to central lab
- Retrospective normalization of local lab data complete for Weeks 1–12

### AE Reporting Compliance
- 5 instances of late AE reporting (>24 hours) identified in review period
- Sites 04 and 14 are repeat offenders — Site 04 was placed on enhanced monitoring in February; non-compliance continues
- Site 09 — first offense; retraining completed

### Concomitant Medication Capture
- New pattern identified: 12 instances of concomitant medications not captured in eCRF
- Review suggests sites are not consistently asking about OTC supplements and herbal products
- 3 participants identified as potentially using St. John's Wort (CYP3A4 inducer) — not captured at screening or during study
- **This represents a new risk not on the current register** — CYP3A4 interaction via undisclosed concomitant medications
